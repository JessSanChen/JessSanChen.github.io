Jessica Chen

I work on AI safety and biosecurity, coming at both from more than one direction.

Some fun things that I’ve built and broke: (1) Spectre, a multi-agent red-teaming system, won $25K at the SCSP AGI Hackathon and jailbroke the Air Force Research Lab’s LLM; (2) co-founding a cybersecurity venture doing automated red-teaming (~$75K ARR) out of the Belfer Center’s QLab accelerator. I also spent too much time on mechanism design for systems whose agents are strategic.

I have also done wet-lab work — including viral circuit tracing at Georgetown Medical School and neural-interface research in Jia Liu’s lab at Harvard.

I’m currently Lead Researcher at AGI House, writing research briefs, interviewing cool people, and hosting research spotlights and quarterly research reading groups. Before that, a year in restructuring at Lazard; before that, Harvard — a dual S.M./A.B. in Computer Science, an A.B. in Economics, and teaching fellow for CS 51 (Intermediate Programming), 136 (Mechanism Design), and 182 (Artificial Intelligenec).
