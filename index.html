Jessica Chen

I work on AI x biosecurity: building the tools and benchmarks that catch AI misuse in the life sciences.

Research

  • AI x biosecurity. As an AI x Biosecurity Fellow at the Cambridge Boston Alignment Initiative, I lead ASTRAL, an open-source scanner for biological misuse. I’m also first author on a benchmark for misuse monitors in dual-use research, with co-authors at NIST and RAND.
  • AI agents. Policy Under Pressure (under review) is a 13-model, 26,398-episode study of how tools, personas, and peer pressure change whether agents follow rules. Agents broke rules over 4× more often through code than through a named tool.
  • Mechanism design. Matching, auctions, and incentives in learning systems (EAAMO 2024).
  • Lab. Viral circuit tracing at Georgetown and electrophysiology pipelines at Harvard. I got hooked on biosecurity as a teenager at Fort Detrick and Walter Reed (more).

Built

  • Spectre, a multi-agent red-teaming system that jailbroke the Air Force Research Lab’s LLM. $25K first prize, SCSP × AGI House hackathon.
  • An expert elicitation platform for the Boston Biosecurity Hackathon, which I founded. It collected decomposed workflows and dual-use comparison sets and handled peer review, with a full admin console. ~60 people used it at once on event day without a failure.
  • CrisisSync, multi-agent disaster response. $4K first prize, AI Agents Hackathon. More builds →

Background

  • Lazard (restructuring) and KKR (credit), where the worst day of a CEO’s career was our normal Tuesday. I was usually the sole analyst on bankruptcies and distressed deals.
  • Harvard: S.M./A.B. in Computer Science, A.B. in Economics. Teaching fellow for CS 182 (Artificial Intelligence), CS 136 (Economics and Computation), and CS 51.
  • AGI House, Lead Researcher: research briefs, podcasts, and the Fellows program.

Elsewhere: born in Taiwan, and I write about the region's political economy: hukou reform and the cross-strait brain drain. I also dance, make digital art, ride motorcycles, and free-dive.

Selected papers

Misuse Monitor Benchmark for Biological and Dual-Use Research DomainsIn progress

J. Chen, C. Fagan, S. Dev, J. Lee, S. Foianini · with co-authors at NIST and RAND · 2026

A benchmark for evaluating AI misuse monitors in dual-use domains. It contributes expert review labels with measured inter-rater agreement, matched conversation sets that hold topic fixed so monitors can't score well on topic alone, and decomposed harm datasets.

Policy Under Pressure: Social Context and Agentic Rule-FollowingUnder review

J. Chen, R. Acharya · Grew out of an Apart Research sprint · 2026

I designed and built the harness for a 13-model, 26,398-episode study of how an agent's tool, operator-assigned persona, and peer reports change whether it follows rules and reports honestly on itself. Agents were more than 4× as likely to break a rule by writing code as by calling a named tool.
