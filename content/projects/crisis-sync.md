**First prize ($4,000), AI Agents Hackathon.** February 2025.

## What it does

During a disaster, the useful signal is buried in a flood of social-media posts
that are duplicated, contradictory, and sometimes false. CrisisSync clusters
incoming alerts into actionable incident reports for authorities, and runs a
**judge/verifier agent** that cross-references each claim against media outlets
before it surfaces.
