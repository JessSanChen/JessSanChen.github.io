The hackathon was a life-sciences expert elicitation study run as a biosecurity
field-building workshop. About 150 people applied for a limited number of seats, and
there were $5,000 in prizes. I recruited participants, secured sponsorship and the
venue, and built the platform the study ran on.

## The platform

The platform is built around LLM conversations, and it collects the kinds of
data a misuse-monitor benchmark needs.

- **Structured data collection.** Participants recorded *decomposed workflows*,
  breaking a task into its steps, and *matched sets* that pair dual-use and
  non-dual-use domains so they can be compared directly.
- **Peer review.** Participants reviewed each other's transcripts. A matching
  process assigned reviewers to submissions.
- **Admin console.** Organizers could customize terms and conditions, stop a
  participant's session, set per-participant token budgets, and see analytics on
  participants and their activity.

## How it went

It was deployed on event day, about 60 participants were on it at the same time,
and nothing broke.
