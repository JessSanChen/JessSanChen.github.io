# Jessica Chen

<img class="portrait" src="files/prof_pic.png" alt="Jessica Chen">

<p class="lede">I work on <strong>AI x biosecurity</strong>: building the tools and benchmarks that catch AI misuse in the life sciences.</p>

**Research**

- **AI x biosecurity.** As an AI x Biosecurity Fellow at the [Cambridge Boston Alignment Initiative](https://www.cbai.ai/), I lead [ASTRAL](https://astral.detection.bio/), an open-source scanner for biological misuse. I'm also first author on a benchmark for misuse monitors in dual-use research, with co-authors at NIST and RAND.
- **AI agents.** [Policy Under Pressure](projects.html#ai-agents-and-evaluation) (under review) is a 13-model, 26,398-episode study of how tools, personas, and peer pressure change whether agents follow rules. Agents broke rules over 4× more often through code than through a named tool.
- **Mechanism design.** Matching, auctions, and incentives in learning systems ([EAAMO 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13083)).
- **Lab.** Viral circuit tracing at Georgetown and electrophysiology pipelines at Harvard. I got hooked on biosecurity as a teenager at Fort Detrick and Walter Reed ([more](projects.html#lab-research)).

**Built**

- **[Spectre](https://github.com/JessSanChen/SpectreRedTeaming)**, a multi-agent red-teaming system that jailbroke the Air Force Research Lab's LLM. **$25K first prize**, SCSP × AGI House hackathon.
- **An [expert elicitation platform](projects/boston-biosecurity-hackathon.html)** for the Boston Biosecurity Hackathon, which I founded. It collected decomposed workflows and dual-use comparison sets and handled peer review, with a full admin console. ~60 people used it at once on event day without a failure.
- **[CrisisSync](https://github.com/JessSanChen/CrisisSync)**, multi-agent disaster response. **$4K first prize**, AI Agents Hackathon. [More builds →](projects.html#builds-and-hackathons)

**Background**

- **Lazard** (restructuring) and **KKR** (credit), where the worst day of a CEO's career was our normal Tuesday. I was usually the sole analyst on bankruptcies and distressed deals.
- **Harvard:** S.M./A.B. in Computer Science, A.B. in Economics. **Teaching fellow** for CS 182 (Artificial Intelligence), CS 136 (Economics and Computation), and CS 51.
- **[AGI House](https://agihouse.org)**, Lead Researcher: [research briefs](writing.html), podcasts, and the Fellows program.

<p class="small muted">Elsewhere: born in Taiwan, and I write about the region's political economy: <a href="files/hukou-reform.pdf">hukou reform</a> and <a href="files/cross-strait-brain-drain.pdf">the cross-strait brain drain</a>. I also dance, make digital art, ride motorcycles, and free-dive.</p>
