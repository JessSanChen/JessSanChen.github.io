# Jessica Chen

<img class="portrait" src="files/prof_pic.png" alt="Jessica Chen">

<p class="lede">I work on <strong>AI x biosecurity</strong>: building the tools and benchmarks that catch AI misuse in the life sciences.</p>

I'm an AI x Biosecurity Fellow at the [Cambridge Boston Alignment Initiative](https://www.cbai.ai/), where I lead [ASTRAL](https://astral.detection.bio/), an open-source scanner for biological misuse. I own the project's data governance and design partnerships. I'm also first author on a benchmark for AI misuse monitors in dual-use research domains, with co-authors at NIST and RAND. This month I founded and ran the [Boston Biosecurity Hackathon](projects.html#ai-x-biosecurity), a life-sciences expert elicitation study and field-building workshop.

More broadly, I study how AI agents behave when their context changes. [Policy Under Pressure](projects.html#ai-agents-and-evaluation) (under review; it grew out of an Apart Research sprint) is a 13-model, 26,398-episode study of how an agent's tools, its operator-assigned persona, and its peers' reports shape whether it follows rules and reports honestly. Before that I worked on [mechanism design](projects.html#mechanism-design-and-learning-systems): what learning and allocation systems do when the agents inside them are strategic.

I've been interested in biosecurity since I was a teenager, when I spent summers at Fort Detrick (culturing bacteria and running PCR to look for *Wolbachia* in local insects) and the Walter Reed Army Institute of Research (immunology work around malaria). After that I did more bench work: brain slicing, patch-clamp recordings, and viral circuit tracing at [Georgetown School of Medicine](projects.html#lab-research). At Harvard I rebuilt the [Jia Liu Lab's](projects.html#lab-research) electrophysiology analysis pipeline.

I'm also Lead Researcher at [AGI House](https://agihouse.org), where I write [research briefs](writing.html), host [podcast interviews](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5KQpZPbeq-Q) and paper reading groups, and started the inaugural [Fellows program](https://www.agihouse.org/fellowship). In a previous life I worked in restructuring banking at Lazard and credit investing at KKR, where the worst day of a CEO's career was our normal Tuesday. At Lazard I was usually the sole analyst on bankruptcies and distressed situations, working daily with management, consultants, and counsel. I studied at Harvard, where I earned an S.M./A.B. in Computer Science and an A.B. in Economics and was a teaching fellow for CS 51, CS 136, and CS 182.

Outside work: I was born in Taiwan (and sometimes write about the political economy of the region [[1]](files/hukou-reform.pdf)[[2]](files/cross-strait-brain-drain.pdf)). I also dance, make digital art, ride motorcycles, and free-dive.
