# Jessica Chen

<img class="portrait" src="files/prof_pic.png" alt="Jessica Chen">

<p class="lede">I work on <strong>AI x biosecurity</strong>: building the tools and benchmarks that catch AI misuse in the life sciences.</p>

**Now**

- **AI x Biosecurity Fellow, [Cambridge Boston Alignment Initiative](https://www.cbai.ai/).** I lead [ASTRAL](https://astral.detection.bio/), an open-source scanner for biological misuse. I'm also first author on a misuse-monitor benchmark with co-authors at NIST and RAND.
- **Founder, [Boston Biosecurity Hackathon](projects.html#ai-x-biosecurity).** An expert elicitation study and field-building workshop: ~150 applicants, $5K in prizes.
- **Lead Researcher, [AGI House](https://agihouse.org).** I write [research briefs](writing.html), host [podcasts](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5KQpZPbeq-Q) and reading groups, and started the [Fellows program](https://www.agihouse.org/fellowship).

**Research**

- **AI agents:** how tools, personas, and peer pressure change agents' rule-following ([Policy Under Pressure](projects.html#ai-agents-and-evaluation), under review).
- **Mechanism design:** matching, auctions, and incentives in learning systems ([EAAMO 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13083)).
- **Lab:** viral circuit tracing at Georgetown and ephys pipelines at Harvard. I got hooked on biosecurity as a teenager at Fort Detrick and Walter Reed ([more](projects.html#lab-research)).

**Previously**

- **Lazard** (restructuring) and **KKR** (credit), where the worst day of a CEO's career was our normal Tuesday. I was usually the sole analyst on bankruptcies and distressed deals.
- **Harvard:** S.M./A.B. in Computer Science, A.B. in Economics.
- **Teaching fellow** for CS 182 (Artificial Intelligence), CS 136 (Economics and Computation), and CS 51.

<p class="small muted">Elsewhere: born in Taiwan, and I write about the region's political economy: <a href="files/hukou-reform.pdf">hukou reform</a> and <a href="files/cross-strait-brain-drain.pdf">the cross-strait brain drain</a>. I also dance, make digital art, ride motorcycles, and free-dive.</p>
