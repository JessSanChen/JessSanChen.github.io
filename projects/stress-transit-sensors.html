STRESS — Distributed Emergency Detection for Train Platforms

Build · CS 262, Harvard · 2023-05

CS 262, Introduction to Distributed Computing. May 2023.

What it does

Cross-platform and intra-rail sensor arrays — Arduino, PIR, and ultrasonic — that detect obstructions on train platforms and propagate alarms across a distributed city transit network over gRPC.

Why it’s here

It’s the systems counterweight to the theory work: consensus, partial failure, and message ordering in a setting where a dropped alarm matters.
